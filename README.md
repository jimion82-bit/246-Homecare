# 246 Home Care

Instant quote and booking page for 246 Home Care, West Coast Barbados.

Visitors choose a service, describe their property and see an estimate while a 3D model of their home builds on screen. Bookings are sent to WhatsApp 246-263-7048.

- `index.html`: the whole site (prices live in the `PRICING` and `PACKAGES` blocks near the top of the script)
- `logo.jpeg`: the 246 Home Care logo
